## 2026-02-28 - Enhanced Feedback & Accessibility in Import/Export
**Learning:** Replacing disruptive browser `alert()` calls with MUI `Snackbar` improves flow, and `Tooltip` components on disabled buttons (wrapped in `<span>`) ensure accessibility even when the action is unavailable.
**Action:** Always prefer non-intrusive notifications and provide contextual help (Tooltips) for destructive or conditional actions.
