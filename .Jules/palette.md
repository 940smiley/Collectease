## 2025-02-25 - Enhance Import/Export UX with Snackbar and Accessibility
**Learning:** Replacing browser 'alert()' with MUI Snackbar improves the flow by providing non-intrusive feedback. Accessibility is greatly improved by adding 'aria-label' to hidden inputs and 'alt' text to images. Visual cues like icons and tooltips help users understand button actions faster.
**Action:** Always prefer Snackbar for feedback and ensure all interactive elements and images have appropriate accessibility attributes.
